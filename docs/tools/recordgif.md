# Record GIF

*Unchanged from stock Bitsy.*

Capture a snapshot or short animation of your game, straight out of the editor — handy for itch.io cover images or social posts.

- **Record** — click to start capturing, click again to stop. The recording is encoded (larger recordings take longer) and shown looping in the preview window once ready. Starting a new recording discards the previous one.
- **Snapshot** — captures a single still frame instead, from either edit or play mode. Also overwrites any existing capture.
- **Size** — square (512×512) or landscape (726×576, padded with the background color) — landscape matches itch.io's cover-image dimensions exactly.
- **Download** — saves the current capture as `recording.gif` to your downloads folder.

Only the most recent capture is kept, and it's lost on page refresh — download before you record something new or reload the page. If the paint tool's pixel grid is visible when you capture, it'll show up in the recording too, so hide it first if you want a clean shot.
