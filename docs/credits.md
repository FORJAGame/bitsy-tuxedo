## Bitsy / Bitsy Color

Bitsy Tuxedo is a fork of Bitsy Color, itself built on Adam Le Doux's original Bitsy. Bitsy Tuxedo doesn't touch the underlying engine or most of the editor, so nearly everyone who's ever contributed to Bitsy is credited for the parts still running under the hood here — programming, documentation, icons, and every other language's translation. See the full, up-to-date list in [`CREDITS.md`](../CREDITS.md) at the repo root.
